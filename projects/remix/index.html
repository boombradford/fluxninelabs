Project Brief

Remix

Remix is a fun new tool still in progress. Remix works as a brand manager for your website. You enter your URL and Remix's job is consistency across your site.

What Remix checks

Remix looks for uniform spacing, fonts, brand colors, and more to flag where your visual system drifts from itself. The goal is clean consistency at scale, page by page.

What Remix enhances

  • AI-assisted graphic upgrades across your website
  • Custom writing engine tuned to your writing style
  • Brand-safe recommendations before publication
  • Consistency enforcement as your content grows
