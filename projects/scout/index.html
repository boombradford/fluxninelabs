Project Brief

Scout

Scout is our AI powered website intelligence tool that audits your website and returns clear, prioritized recommendations. We provide this audit to anyone for free, and if you want to address the issues you find, our team is here to help.

How Scout works

In the background, Scout uses Google My Places data, Google Lighthouse, and COT prompting to identify local competitors, compare your website against theirs, factor a score, and generate recommendations you can act on immediately.

What it delivers

  • Website audit with weighted scoring
  • Local competition comparison
  • Free recommendations with clear priorities
  • Optional implementation support from our team
